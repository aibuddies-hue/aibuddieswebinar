Privacy Policy

Last updated: —

This policy explains what collects when you register for the AI Creator Summit, why it is collected, and who else sees it. It applies to this website and to the registration form on it.

What we collect

When you register, you give us:

  • Your name, mobile number and email address.
  • The professional segment you pick from the form (creator, freelancer, business owner and so on).

The website also records, on its own:

  • How you reached the page — the campaign parameters in the link, the site that referred you, and the advertising click identifiers that Facebook and Google add to their own links.
  • Whether you are on a mobile, tablet or desktop device.
  • The exact page address you landed on.

We do not collect card numbers, UPI IDs, bank details or any other payment credentials. Payment is handled entirely by Razorpay on their own page; we only learn whether a payment succeeded.

Why we collect it

  • To reserve your seat and send you the session link and reminders.
  • To add you to the WhatsApp group where the session details are shared.
  • To understand which campaigns and creatives bring registrations, so we do not waste money on ones that do not.
  • To contact you about this session and future sessions we run.

Who else sees it

Your details are stored in a private Google Sheet (Google LLC) that only our team can open. Beyond that:

  • Meta Pixel records that a registration happened, so ad delivery can be measured. Meta receives the event, not your form answers.
  • Razorpay receives what it needs to process your payment, under its own privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp is where the session link and reminders are sent, so your number is visible to the group in the normal way WhatsApp groups work.

We do not sell your details, and we do not hand them to unrelated third parties.

What is stored in your browser

The page keeps a small amount of information in your own browser's local storage: your registration record so the confirmation page can greet you and not ask twice, how you arrived, and whether you have already dismissed the group invite. It never leaves your device except as described above, and clearing your browser data removes it.

How long we keep it

Registration records are kept for as long as we run these sessions and may contact you about them. Ask us to delete yours and we will, unless we are required to keep a payment record for tax or accounting purposes.

Your choices

Write to us at the address below to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. You can leave the WhatsApp group at any time, and ask to be removed from future messages.

Children

This session is meant for working adults. We do not knowingly collect details from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new date at the top.
